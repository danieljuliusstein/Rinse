Rinse Setup Onboarding

2026-07-01 · Welcome → Auth → 6-step onboarding (post iOS polish)

Council Verdict

Keep the iOS UI language. Cut perceived length and configuration — optimize for “see your invoice” and “share your link,” not completeness.

  • Keep: Settings rows, section labels, footnotes, grouped cards, frosted footer.
  • Fix: 6-step + mini-screen fatigue; sample invoice anxiety; weak booking share moment; welcome/onboarding tone shift.
  • Defer: inline package editing, template gallery depth, operator app shell.
The one thing to do first

Collapse business step to one grouped card + add per-step completion analytics before bigger restructuring.

Where the council agrees (full)

iOS row inputs, section labels, and footnotes are the right mobile pattern for non-technical owners. The flow is still too long for phone-between-jobs context. The first-invoice preview is the core product moment but arrives too late. Welcome (Syne marketing) and onboarding (Settings forms) feel like two apps. Terms like packages, templates, and sample invoice confuse fresh users.

Where the council clashes

Step count: First Principles wants 2–3 moments; Executor says keep 6 slugs but collapse screens and defer optional fields.

Services: Defer all editing vs read-only defaults with edit-in-settings link.

Plans step: Contrarian wants it gone or pre-app; Executor wants better CTA copy only.

Chairman resolution: Don't rewrite routing yet — reduce perceived screens, add analytics, then decide.

Blind spots from peer review

Double progress (6-bar + mini-dots). Sample invoice may create real records — users fear sending to clients. Booking step needs Copy/Share as primary UI. Resume-after-abandon UX. Logo upload on slow networks. Right-aligned inputs truncating long business names.

Contrarian Full response

iOS polish fixed inputs not length. Six steps + three business mini-screens = 8+ screens before unlock. Sample invoice is homework with API calls. Welcome Syne vs onboarding Settings is a tone cliff. Six-segment progress signals long setup. Plans step after five config screens feels bait-and-switch. Step 2 (edit 4 packages) and step 4 (fake records) will drive drop-off.

First Principles Full response

Job-to-be-done: enough data to invoice, proof app works, one shareable win. Not template galleries or four package descriptions on day zero. Right shape: business name + phone → invoice with their name → booking link. Keep iOS UI language; cut information architecture. Defer logo, accent, address, service editing to in-app settings.

Expansionist Full response

Invoice preview is the emotional peak — show it within 60s using auth business name. Booking link should end with native share + suggested caption. Services could use smart regional defaults instead of editing forms. iOS trust aesthetic is a moat if the flow is shorter and ends with a textable artifact.

Outsider Full response

Sample invoice scary — will someone get billed? Why edit four services first? Six green bars = long wait. "Try Rinse free" confusing after signup. iOS forms feel legit. Expected: name, phone, price, show customer view, share link. Jargon: packages, templates, accent colors vs "text a price and get paid."

Executor Full response

1–2 day fixes: unify welcome/onboarding typography; one business card not three mini-screens; services read-only defaults; pre-fill sample client + preview banner; booking Copy/Share above fold; plans CTA "Enter Rinse." Don't touch operator shell or OAuth. Add step analytics immediately.

Recommended next sprint (priority order)
  1. Merge business mini-screens → one card
  2. Step completion analytics
  3. Services: read-only defaults
  4. First invoice: pre-fill + "nothing sent" banner
  5. Booking: Copy + Share primary
  6. Invoice look: default pre-selected, customize later
  7. Plans: rename primary CTA
